SUMMARY: The FAA proposes to supersede Airworthiness Directive (AD) 2024-05-06, which applies to all Leonardo S.p.a Model AW169 helicopters. Since the FAA issued AD 2024-05-06, it was determined that incorrect material was referenced to revise the ALS.
